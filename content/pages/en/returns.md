If a piece isn't right, you can return it within **30 days** of delivery.

## What can be returned

- Unused pieces in their original condition and packaging.
- Pieces that arrived damaged or not as described (we cover the return shipping).

Made-to-order and personalised pieces can only be returned when they are faulty.

## How it works

1. Write to [{{email}}](mailto:{{email}}) with your order number and the piece you want to return.
2. We send you the return address and, for a faulty piece, a prepaid label.
3. Pack the piece the way it arrived and hand it to the carrier.

## Refunds

The refund goes back to the payment method you used, within 5 working days of the return arriving at the workshop. Original shipping costs are refunded when the piece was faulty.

## Exchanges

We don't exchange; return the piece and place a new order for the one you want.
