{{brand}} is a small shop for objects made in small batches. The pieces come from independent workshops; each one ships from the bench that made it.

## How it works

We don't keep a warehouse. When you order, the maker packs the piece and sends it to you directly. You get a tracking link, the maker gets a fair price, and nothing sits on a shelf for months.

## What we look for

Materials that age well: stoneware, solid timber, linen, wool. Work that is made to be used every day. Makers who sign their work.

## The makers

Every piece says who made it. [Meet the makers](/makers).
