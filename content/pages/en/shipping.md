## Where we ship

We ship within {{country}} and to most countries worldwide. The delivery options and their prices are shown at checkout once you enter your address.

## Tracking

You receive a tracking link by e-mail as soon as the parcel is on its way. It can take a day before the carrier shows the first scan.

## Duties and taxes

International parcels may be charged import duties or taxes by the destination country. These are not included in the price and are paid by the recipient on delivery.
