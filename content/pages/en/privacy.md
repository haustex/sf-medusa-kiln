This page explains what data {{brand}} collects when you use this site, why, and what you can do about it. Replace it with your own policy before going live.

## What we collect

- **Account and order data** — name, e-mail, address, phone and the pieces you ordered. We need these to deliver and to help you afterwards.
- **Payment data** — handled by our payment provider; we never see your full card number.
- **Site usage** — a cookie keeps your basket between visits. We don't run advertising trackers.

## Why we keep it

To fulfil your order, to answer your questions, to meet accounting and tax rules, and — only if you asked for it — to send news from the studio.

## Who sees it

The maker who ships your order (name and address), the carrier, and our payment and e-mail providers. Nobody else, unless the law requires it.

## Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, correct it, or have it deleted, by writing to [{{email}}](mailto:{{email}}). Order records are kept as long as accounting rules require.
